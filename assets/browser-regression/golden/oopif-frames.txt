Cross-origin iframe fixture
Each frame below is a separate origin, so Chrome runs it out of process and it
  is reached through its own CDP session. Interactive elements sit next to and
  on top of the frames: hit testing at those points is where a page-level hit
  test (which pierces into the child frame) and a document-level one (which
  stops at the iframe element) disagree.
